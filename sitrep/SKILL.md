---
name: sitrep
description: explain briefly to human what you've done
---

Assume the reader hasn't read or followed anything you've done (since previous sitrep if there was one). Don't assume the reader already knows what you're talking about. Don't start the first sentence with "the run" (what run? what are you talking about?). Assume the human has 0 context and blank slate understanding.

Summarise:  
What was the task given to you.  
How did you go about executing the task.  
What have you achieved.  
What do you plan to do but haven't done yet.  
Any obstacles or open questions.  

Max 1000 characters.
